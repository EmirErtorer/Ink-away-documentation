---
title: "Make room"
description: "Move a book's text out of the way to make space for stickers, pictures and notes."
---

Make room pushes a book's text aside so you have space to write, paste a picture or
put a sticker. The font stays the same, and the book's file is never changed.

## Make a room

1. In annotation mode, **hold the Lasso button** and pick **Make room**. The button keeps it, so next time it is one tap away. Hold again to switch back.
2. **Draw a box** on the page. The page shades the exact space it will free.
3. Move the box or drag its corners until it's right, then make the room.

You can also select something you already put on the page (a sticker, picture,
shape or writing) and choose **Make room** from its menu. The text moves out of its
way at once.

## How rooms behave

- A box at a side frees the space from that side of the text to the box's far edge. A wide box goes across the page.
- A room keeps its size whatever the font or zoom, and what you put in it stays in it when the text moves, even to another page.
- **Tap a room** with the tool to resize or remove it.
- The Lasso's settings list the rooms in the book, and have a switch to hide them all.
- The first room in a book loads the book again once. Every room after that is quick.

## Exact lines

By default a room starts at the beginning of a paragraph. Turn on **Exact lines** in
the Lasso's settings to start it on the very line you drew on.

To do that, Ink Away works on a private copy of the book in its own cache folder.
The book's file is never changed, moved or sent, so reading progress sync still
matches. **Back to the original book**, in the same settings, removes it again.

## Where it doesn't work

Make room is for reflowing books (EPUB and the like). It isn't offered on a PDF or
other fixed-page book, in continuous scroll, or with two pages side by side.
