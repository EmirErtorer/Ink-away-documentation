## A notebook for each book

Every book gets a notebook of its own, with a page for each chapter. While reading,
**swipe up the right edge** to open it. It opens in a window over the page, which
you can expand to full screen.

Book notes are full notebooks: every pen, layer and tool works, and they show in
search, export and cloud sync.

## Handwritten notes on highlights

1. Select some words in KOReader.
2. Pick **Handwritten note** in KOReader's menu, next to Add note.
3. The words are highlighted, and a small writing window opens. Write your note.

The note is a page of the book's notes, titled with the highlighted words. Tap the
highlight later to see the note and write more. The window's strip ends in a
**Highlight** button for KOReader's menu of that highlight (delete, style,
colour).

You can also add one from the colour sheet that opens when you tap a highlight in
annotation mode.

In KOReader's lists, the highlight shows as having a note: "(Handwritten note)",
unless you also typed one.
