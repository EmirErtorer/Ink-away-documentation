## Start

Open a book and **swipe down the right edge**. This is annotation mode: Ink Away's
toolbar comes up over the page, and pen, eraser, shapes, text, pictures and
stickers all work on it. Close it with the X.

It works on EPUB, MOBI, FB2, PDF, DjVu, CBZ and every other format KOReader opens.

## Ink follows the text

- **On a reflowing book** (EPUB and the like), each stroke is anchored to the words beside it. Change the font size, margins or orientation and the ink moves with its words.
- **On a PDF or comic**, ink keeps its place on the page.
- **Pin to this page:** select something with the lasso and choose **Pin to this page** to keep it at the same spot on the same page number instead. **Let it follow the text** undoes that.

## The toolbar

Put it on any side of the screen, fold it away, or fit it in the book's margin,
clear of the text. Its settings are under **Book ink** on the toolbar.

Turn pages with the page turn buttons or the arrows on the toolbar.

## Bookmarks

Long press the page number at the bottom of the screen to bookmark the page (as a
KOReader bookmark). Long press again to remove it. This works with any tool in
hand and needs KOReader's footer to be on.

## Without opening annotation mode

On a stylus with buttons, the eraser end erases and holding the side button
highlights, even while you are just reading.

## Where it's kept

The ink lives beside the book's own KOReader data and follows the book when you
move or rename it. Deleting it puts it in Ink Away's trash for 30 days. The book's
file itself is never changed.
