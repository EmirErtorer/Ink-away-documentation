## Smart highlighter

In annotation mode, a highlighter stroke over text becomes **KOReader's own
highlight**, in the pen's colour. It shows in your highlights list like any other.

- Strokes that wobble or run into the margin still snap to the words.
- Over empty space, the highlighter stays ink.
- The eraser takes highlights away like ink, and a long press on one offers to remove it.

## Change a highlight's colour

Tap a highlight in annotation mode (with a navigating finger, the lasso or Pan, or
a single tap with the highlighter) to open a sheet of colours.

- The list is yours: hold a colour to remove it, add your own from the colour wheel, or **Reset colours**.
- One undo takes the change back, and exports follow the new colour.

## Labels

Say what each colour means to you, such as "Important" or "Look this up", with
**Labels** in the same sheet. Labels sync with your other settings.

## Handwritten notes on highlights

Select words in KOReader and pick **Handwritten note**, next to Add note. See
[Book notes](/docs/books/book-notes#handwritten-notes-on-highlights).

## Not drawn on a viewer plugin's page

On a page shown by another plugin (such as Panels+), KOReader's highlights are
drawn by KOReader, and the eraser and colour sheet don't reach them there.
