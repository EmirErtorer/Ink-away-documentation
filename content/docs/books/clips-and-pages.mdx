## Screen clips

Copy a rectangle of a book page, a diagram or a quote, and paste it into a notebook.

1. Start a clip in one of these ways:
   - in annotation mode, **Image**, then **Clip the page**,
   - KOReader's **Tools**, **Ink Away**, **Copy part of the screen**,
   - a two-finger swipe from the top left to the bottom right while reading.
2. Drag the pen, or tap two opposite corners.
3. Check the outlined area, then confirm. Or select again, or cancel.
4. Open a notebook and paste the clip with the lasso or the page menu.

Clips you don't use are deleted after a week from the `screen clips` folder. In
that folder, the library's **...** button changes how long they're kept (1, 3, 7,
14 or 30 days), turns this off, or deletes them all now. A clip you paste is copied
first, so it stays.

## Two pages side by side

For a PDF, CBZ or other fixed-page book, turn on two pages side by side in the
book's Ink Away menu: always, or only in landscape. The first page can stay on its
own if it is a cover, and you can turn it on for every PDF. Ink stays on the page it
was drawn on.
