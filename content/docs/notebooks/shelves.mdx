With the [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin) plugin
installed, your notebooks and drawings can each have a shelf of their own.
SolarUI's notebook shelf shows them too.

## Notebooks shelf

Every notebook stands face out on its cover: Front page, Hardcover, Composition
book, Reporter pad, Kraft with window or Legal pad, in its tab colour or one you
pick.

## Drawings shelf

Every drawing hangs in a frame, from Gilded Baroque, Louis XVI and Art Deco to
Museum Mat, Painted Box, Canvas Wrap and Instant Photo, each in a few finishes. A
new drawing gets one at random.

## Tap and hold

- **Tap** a notebook or drawing to open it in Ink Away, on the page you left.
- **Hold** it to pick its cover or frame. Its menu in the library does the same.

Covers are pictures Ink Away saves when you close a notebook or drawing, so the
shelf opens at once.

## Bookshelf ornaments

With Bookshelf installed, you can also save a drawing straight into its ornaments
from Export.
