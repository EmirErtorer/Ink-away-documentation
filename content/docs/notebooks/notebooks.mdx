A notebook has as many pages as you like. Each page has a title, a star and its
own paper. Make one with **File**, **New notebook**.

## Pages

- Insert, duplicate and move pages from the page menu.
- Save a page as a template, to start new pages from it.
- Turn pages with a two-finger swipe left or right, or the page turn buttons. Hold Prev or Next to jump to the first or last page.
- A notebook opens on the page you left it on.

## Papers and planners

Blank, lined, grid, dotted, isometric, margin ruled and Cornell, plus handwriting
practice, checklist, two columns, storyboard, music, daily, weekly, week columns,
monthly, meeting notes and a habit tracker.

You set the line spacing and how dark the lines are. Drawings can use any of these
papers as a guide too.

## Paper colours

![Paper colours](/screenshots/paper-colours.png)

Twelve papers on colour screens, from cream and legal pad to kraft, blueprint and
chalkboard, and white or black on grey screens. On a dark paper, black ink and text
turn white. Exports come out on the same paper.

## Links and contents

![A contents page](/screenshots/contents-page.png)

- **Links:** lasso anything and make it a link to a page of this notebook or another one. A Back button takes you back. Follow a link by tapping it with Pan, or with a finger while palm rejection is on.
- **Contents page:** lists the pages that have a title, each one a link. **Go to the contents page** in the page menu (or on a gesture) gets you there.

## Handwriting in search

Text you typed, and handwriting you converted to text, can be found with the
library's search. See [Library](/docs/notebooks/library#search).
