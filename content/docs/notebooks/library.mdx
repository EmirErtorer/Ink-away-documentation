![The library](/screenshots/library.png)

Every drawing and notebook is a file that saves itself. Open the library with the
**Library** button on the toolbar.

## Folders

- Make folders, and rename, move, duplicate and delete anything, without a computer.
- **Hold a folder** to give it one of twelve colours and an icon (from KOReader's icons, or your own `koreader/icons` folder).
- The top bar shows where you are, like Library › 2026 › School.
- Pictures in a folder show too, and open full screen.

## Browse

![Browse](/screenshots/browse.png)

A folder's notebooks as coloured tabs beside their pages, so you see every page at
a glance. Show only starred pages, or move and copy pages to any notebook.

Open it with a long two-finger swipe up in a notebook.

## Search

![Search](/screenshots/search.png)

Search by name, or by the text inside pages, including handwriting you converted
to text.

## Trash

Deleted notebooks, drawings, folders and pages wait in the trash for 30 days.
Restore one and it goes back exactly where it was.
