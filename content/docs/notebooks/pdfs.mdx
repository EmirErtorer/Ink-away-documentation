---
title: "PDFs as notebooks"
description: "Open a PDF as a notebook and write on its pages."
---

![A PDF opened as a notebook](/screenshots/pdf-annotation.png)

Any PDF can become a notebook: a worksheet, a form, a score, a printed planner.

## Open one

- **From KOReader's file browser:** long press the PDF and choose **Open as Ink Away notebook**.
- **From your phone or computer:** send it over Wi-Fi (see [Send over Wi-Fi](/docs/share/wifi)). It arrives in Library, Imported, as a notebook.

Then write on it like any notebook: every pen, layer and tool works.

## Get it back out

Export it as a normal PDF with your ink, or as an **editable PDF**: a copy of the
original with your ink added as real PDF annotations, which any PDF app can edit or
remove. See [Export](/docs/share/export#editable-pdf).

## Reading a PDF instead

If you only want to make notes while reading a PDF in KOReader, you don't need a
notebook. Open it as a book and [annotate it](/docs/books/annotate). The ink stays
with the book.
