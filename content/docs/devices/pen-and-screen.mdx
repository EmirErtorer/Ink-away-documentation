## Palm rejection

Rest your hand on the screen while the pen writes. The pen can still tap the
toolbar and menus. It is on by default on the Kindle Scribe and reMarkable, and
needs KOReader 2026.07 or newer.

**Finger on the page**, in the pen settings, decides what a finger does while it's
on: **Navigate** (scroll, turn pages, hold for menus) or **Nothing**.

## Stylus buttons

On pens that have them, the eraser end erases and holding the side button turns the
pen into a highlighter. Both work while reading too, without opening annotation
mode. Change them in Settings, **Gestures and pen buttons**.

## Dark mode

![Dark mode](/screenshots/dark-mode.png)

Ink Away's toolbars, menus and library can be Light, Dark, or follow KOReader's
night mode (System, the default). Your page and your colours are never changed.

## Colour screens

A theme colour (Ink Away green unless you pick another) and colour ink that shows
as you draw. In KOReader's night mode, ink keeps its colour: blue stays blue, and
dark ink turns light so you can read it on the dark page.

## E-ink refreshes

- Light, fast refreshes while you draw.
- An optional clean-up refresh every few strokes.
- A full refresh whenever you like, from Settings or with a long two-finger swipe down.
- Portrait or landscape, and bars that fold away for more room.
