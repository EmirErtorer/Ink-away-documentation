## The right edge swipe doesn't open Ink Away

Your reader may already use the right edge, for its light for example. Try the
two-finger swipe from the bottom right corner toward the top left, or open Ink Away
from the top menu, **Tools** tab. See [Gestures](/docs/start/gestures) to set your
own.

## Ink Away isn't in the Tools menu

- Check the folder is called exactly `ink-away.koplugin`, and sits directly in `plugins` (not inside another folder from the zip).
- Restart KOReader after copying it.

## My palm draws on the page

- Palm rejection needs KOReader 2026.07 or newer.
- Set **Finger on the page** to **Nothing** in the pen settings, so a finger never draws.
- If another pen plugin is or was installed, see [Other pen plugins](/docs/devices/devices#other-pen-plugins).

## Ghosting or grey leftovers on the screen

Do a long two-finger swipe down for a full refresh, or turn on the clean-up refresh
every few strokes in Settings.

## Something is hard to find

The **Guide** in Settings has a short card for each topic, each with **Show me**.

## Report a bug

Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/issues/new/choose).
These help the most:

1. **Your reader, KOReader version and Ink Away version** (Ink Away's is in Settings, Updates).
2. **The steps** that make it happen, one by one.
3. **The crash log**, if KOReader closed. Right after it happens, connect the reader to a computer and copy `crash.log`:

   | Reader | Where |
   | --- | --- |
   | Kindle | `koreader/crash.log` |
   | Kobo | `.adds/koreader/crash.log` |
   | Android | `koreader/crash.log` in the app's storage |

4. **A photo or short video** of the screen.

For pen and touch problems, Settings has **Test pen and touch**, which shows how
your pen, its buttons and your hand arrive. A photo of it helps a lot.
