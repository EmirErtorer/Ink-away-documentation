## Open Ink Away

**Swipe up the right edge of the screen** in KOReader's file browser. The first
time, Ink Away opens on a blank drawing.

You can also open it from KOReader's top menu (**Tools** tab, **Ink Away (drawing
canvas)**) or from Ink Away's icon in KOReader's top bar, which opens a quick menu.

If your reader already uses the right edge, for its light for example, Ink Away
gives you a two-finger swipe instead: from the bottom right corner toward the top
left. It tells you which one you got.

## The toolbar

![The toolbar](/assets/readme-toolbar.svg)

From left to right: Pen, Eraser, Shapes, Text, Image, Lasso, Undo, Redo,
Settings, Library, File and Exit.

**Tap a tool to pick it, and tap it again for its settings**: size, colour, kind
of pen and so on. File holds rename, duplicate, export, new drawing and new
notebook. Zoom is the floating + and − in the corner, with the Pan button above it.

## Make a notebook

1. Tap **File**, then **New notebook**.
2. Pick a paper: lined, grid, dotted, a planner page and so on.
3. Write. Turn pages with a two-finger swipe left or right, or the page turn buttons.

Everything saves by itself. There is no Save button.

## Write on a book

1. **Open a book** in KOReader as usual.
2. **Swipe down the right edge.** The toolbar comes up, and you write right on the page.
3. **Close with the X.** Your ink stays with the book and shows the next time you read it.

On a reader that uses the right edge, the two-finger swipe goes from the top right
toward the bottom left. See [Books](/docs/books/annotate) for everything you can do
on a book.

## Find your way

- **The Guide** in Settings has a short card for anything a glance doesn't show, each with **Show me**.
- **Settings, When Ink Away opens** picks what you see first: the last document, the library, or your notebooks.
- Drawings and notebooks are kept in the `ink away` folder. Exports go to `ink away/exports` unless you pick another folder.
