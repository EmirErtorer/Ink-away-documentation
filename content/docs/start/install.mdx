---
title: "Install"
description: "Put Ink Away on your reader."
---

You need KOReader already installed on your reader. If you don't have it yet, start
with [KOReader's own guide](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/wiki).

## Steps

1. **Download** `ink-away.koplugin-vX.Y.Z.zip` (X.Y.Z is the version number) from the [latest release](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/releases/latest), under Assets.
2. **Unzip it** on your computer. You get a folder called `ink-away.koplugin`.
3. **Connect your reader** to the computer with a USB cable.
4. **Copy the `ink-away.koplugin` folder** into KOReader's `plugins` folder:

   | Reader | Folder |
   | --- | --- |
   | Kindle | `koreader/plugins/` |
   | Kobo | `.adds/koreader/plugins/` (`.adds` is hidden: turn on hidden files on your computer) |
   | Android (Boox and others) | `koreader/plugins/` in the app's storage |

5. **Eject the reader and restart KOReader.**

To check it worked, open the top menu, **Tools** tab: you should see **Ink Away (drawing canvas)**.

## Updating

After the first install, Ink Away updates itself. Open its **Settings**, then
**Updates**. See [Updates](/docs/devices/updates) for prereleases and the automatic check.

To update by hand instead, repeat the steps above and replace the old folder.
Your drawings and notebooks are kept elsewhere, so nothing is lost.

## Removing it

Delete the `ink-away.koplugin` folder from `plugins`. Your work stays in the
`ink away` folder, inside KOReader's own folder, until you delete that too.
