---
title: "The surface"
description: "The table that tells Ink Away where the book's page is on your viewer's screen."
---

A surface is a Lua table you write. You pass it to
[installPluginIntegration](/docs/developers/api/install-plugin-integration), and Ink Away
uses it to place ink on your view and to save what is drawn there as ink on the
book's own pages.

The [rendering example](/docs/developers/examples/rendering-consumer) shows a small
working surface. This page is the full contract.

## Surface contract

```lua
local surface = {
    version = 1,
    document = ui.document, -- the open document itself, not just the same file
}
```

Screen coordinates are absolute pixels. Page coordinates are the document page's
own units.

| Required method | Accepts | Returns or does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pages()` | Nothing | The document pages shown, in display order. |
| `key()` | Nothing | A string that changes whenever where a page sits on the screen changes. Ink Away caches the placed ink by it, so keep it exact and cheap. |
| `toPage(x, y)` | Screen point | `page, px, py, zoom, angle`, or `nil`. |
| `toScreen(page, px, py)` | Page point | `x, y, zoom, angle`, or `nil` when that page is not shown. |
| `clip()` | Nothing | The page's part of the screen, `{x, y, w, h}`, without your controls or margins. Ink is drawn and painted only there. |
| `snapshot(bb)` | Screen-sized buffer | Paint your view into `bb` **without** the ink. Annotation mode shows it under the canvas. |
| `repaint()` | Nothing | Ask for your view to be painted again. |

- `zoom` is screen pixels per page unit and must be positive. `angle` is clockwise, in radians. Both mappings must agree.
- Keep the mappings valid outside `clip()`: clipping is separate from mapping.
- Never call `paintDocumentSurface` from `snapshot`, or the ink is painted into its own background.
- Never keep or free the buffer passed to `snapshot`.

## Optional callbacks

A basic viewer can leave these out. Add the ones your viewer needs. Any you supply
must be functions.

| Method | What it does | When left out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `begin()` | Annotation mode is opening on this surface. Hide your controls here. | Nothing extra happens. |
| `finish()` | Annotation mode has closed on it. Show your controls again. It must cope with your viewer already being closed. | Nothing extra happens. |
| `turn(dir, done, frame)` | Turn the page: `1` forward, `-1` back. Call `done(next_surface)` once it has settled. Pass the new surface when the page's surface changed, or nothing to stay on this one. Call `frame()` after each animation step. | Page turns stay on the page. |
| `readingDirection()` | `"ltr"` or `"rtl"`. On a right-to-left page, the toolbar's left arrow goes forward. | The reader's setting. |
| `setViewport(rect, change)` | The room annotation mode leaves beside its toolbar. Return `true` if your page moved. Keep `change` for later: see below. | Your layout stays as it is. |
| `regions()` | The parts of the page, each a surface of its own with its own `clip()`, such as the two halves of a spread with the gutter removed. | The whole surface is painted as one. |
| `mapPath(points, to_page, page)` and `isAffine()` | For a page one transform cannot map: map a flat `{x1, y1, x2, y2, ...}` path point by point (`to_page` true maps screen to page), and return `false` from `isAffine()`. A shape that crosses the fold is kept as a polygon. | One transform maps everything. |

### Page turns

Ink Away saves what is in hand before asking you to turn, and drawing waits until
you call `done`. Keep the old mappings working while the page moves. The surface you
pass to `done` must belong to the same document. An invalid one is reported to your
`on_error`, and annotation mode stays on the old surface.

### Moving the page while annotating

Before you move or resize the page yourself, call the latest `change` with a
function that does the move: `change(function() ... end)`. Ink Away keeps what is
in hand against the old layout, runs your function, then reloads against the new
one. Don't call `change` from inside `setViewport`, and drop it in `finish()`.

## Known limits

- Only fixed-page books (PDF, comics, DjVu). A surface cannot map reflowing books such as EPUB.
- KOReader's own highlights aren't drawn on your page, and the eraser and highlight colour sheet don't reach them there.
- While your page animates, annotation mode shows its picture and takes no input.
