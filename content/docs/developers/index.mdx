Ink Away has a small public API for other KOReader plugins. With it, your plugin
can work with Ink Away without touching its code or patching it at runtime.

<Callout type="warn" title="Experimental">
The API ships with Ink Away 4.4.3 and newer. It may change before Ink Away 5.0.
Check the [version numbers](/docs/developers/api/integration-api-version) before
using it, and leave your plugin working as usual if they aren't the ones you know.
</Callout>

## What you can do with it

There are two ways to use the API. Most plugins need only the first.

- **Listen for annotation mode.** Know when the reader starts and stops annotating, for example to pause your own timers, hide an overlay or save your state. You use two events, `onAnnotationOpened` and `onAnnotationClosed`. See [Listen for annotation mode](/docs/developers/examples/event-only).
- **Annotate your own viewer.** Let readers write on pages **your** plugin shows: zoomed panels, a custom layout, a two-page spread. You describe your screen with a surface and connect it with `installPluginIntegration`. See [Annotate your own viewer](/docs/developers/examples/rendering-consumer).

You don't need to know anything about Ink Away's internals for either. Ink Away
keeps its own tools, toolbar, saving and cloud sync; your plugin only answers a few
questions about its own screen.

## Example: Panels+

<video controls playsInline preload="metadata" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fdf3ef83-c7df-48f4-bb30-ca32a2305b90"></video>

[Panels+](https://github.com/KristanLaimon/PanelsPlus) reads comics panel by panel:
it shows one panel at a time, zoomed to fill the screen, with its own renderer. That
is exactly the case KOReader's normal view can't handle. If Ink Away drew on the
screen as usual, the ink would be saved where it lands on the **screen**, not on the
**page**, and it would end up in the wrong place as soon as the reader left the
panel.

The API fixes that. This is what happens in the video:

1. **The reader opens a comic in Panels+** and moves to a panel. Panels+ has already connected to Ink Away with `installPluginIntegration`, giving it a **surface**: a table that says which page is shown, and how a point on the screen maps to a point on that page.
2. **The reader taps "Draw (Ink-away)"** on Panels+'s control rail. Panels+ calls `ink:onInkAwayAnnotate()`.
3. **Ink Away opens annotation mode over the panel**, with its usual toolbar. It asks the surface for the panel's area on the screen (`clip`), so its drawing area covers the panel and not Panels+'s controls. A viewer can hide its own controls here, in the surface's optional `begin()`.
4. **The reader writes on the panel.** Every stroke is turned into page coordinates through the surface's `toPage`. A panel zoomed to twice its size gives a stroke half the size on the page, in the same place.
5. **The reader taps the toolbar's `>` arrow.** Ink Away saves what is in hand and calls the surface's `turn(1, done)`. Panels+ moves to the next panel, which may be on the next page, and calls `done(next_surface)` with the new panel's surface. Drawing waits until then.
6. **The reader closes annotation mode.** The surface's optional `finish()` is where a viewer shows its controls again. From then on, Panels+ paints the saved ink on its panels with `ink:paintDocumentSurface(surface, bb)` in its own paint function.
7. **The reader goes back to KOReader's normal page view.** The ink is there, at the right spot and size, because it was saved on the page, not on the screen. It also syncs and exports like any other ink.

Panels+ needs a build with Ink Away support. Its stable 1.6.0 release doesn't have it yet.

## How it fits together

Whichever way you use the API, connecting works the same way:

1. **When a book opens**, Ink Away puts its API on the reader as `ui.inkAwayIntegrationAPI`, once it is ready.
2. **Your plugin checks for it.** KOReader loads plugins in name order, so Ink Away may not be ready yet when your plugin starts. If the API is there, use it at once. If not, add your plugin to `ui.inkAwayPendingIntegrations` and Ink Away calls your plugin's `configureInkAway(ink)` as soon as it is ready. See [the ready instance](/docs/developers/api/ready-instance) and [the pending queue](/docs/developers/api/pending-integrations).
3. **In `configureInkAway(ink)`**, check the version numbers, then subscribe to events or connect your viewer.
4. **When your plugin or viewer closes**, call the functions you got back (the unsubscribes, or the `cleanup` from `installPluginIntegration`).
5. **When the book closes**, Ink Away removes `ui.inkAwayIntegrationAPI`, and the old table refuses new connections.

## The API at a glance

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `integration_api_version`, `document_surface_version` | Both `1` for now. Check them before anything else |
| `ui` | The reader this API belongs to |
| [`onAnnotationOpened(cb)`](/docs/developers/api/on-annotation-opened), [`onAnnotationClosed(cb)`](/docs/developers/api/on-annotation-closed) | Call `cb` when annotation mode opens or closes, on any page. Each returns an unsubscribe function |
| [`installPluginIntegration(widget, surface, options)`](/docs/developers/api/install-plugin-integration) | Connect your viewer and its [surface](/docs/developers/api/surface). Returns a `cleanup` function |
| [`paintDocumentSurface(surface, bb)`](/docs/developers/api/paint-document-surface) | Paint the book's saved ink on your page, in your own paint function |
| [`releaseDocumentSurface(surface)`](/docs/developers/api/release-document-surface) | Close annotation mode on your surface, keeping the ink |
| [`onInkAwayAnnotate()`](/docs/developers/api/annotate) | Open annotation mode: on your viewer if it is connected and shown, otherwise on KOReader's page |

## What Ink Away promises

- **Nothing changes for anyone not using your plugin.** KOReader's own page works exactly as before: same refreshes, pen, keys and exports.
- **Your failures stay yours.** Every call into your surface is guarded. A failure is logged and passed to your `on_error`, and annotation mode falls back to KOReader's page, or closes, keeping the ink.
- **One ink format.** Ink drawn on your view is stored per page, in page units, exactly like ink drawn in KOReader's view. A book annotated in your plugin opens with its ink in KOReader, on another reader and in exports.
- **No copies of the screen.** Painting on your page draws only the ink, and only inside your clip.

## Limits

- **Fixed-page books only.** A surface maps PDF, comic (CBZ, CBR) and DjVu pages. Reflowing books such as EPUB can't be shown through a surface.
- **KOReader's highlights** aren't drawn on your page, since KOReader's own view draws them. On your page, Ink Away's highlighter draws see-through ink instead.
- **While your page animates** during a turn, annotation mode shows a picture of it and takes no input.
