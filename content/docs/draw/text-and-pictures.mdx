## Text boxes

Pick **Text** and tap the page. The on-screen keyboard comes up.

- Any font installed in KOReader, with bold, italic, underline, sizes, alignment and line spacing.
- Lists, and checklists you tick with a tap.
- On a notebook's lines, the text snaps to the ruling.
- On a colour screen, coloured text and highlights.
- Drag the corner grip to make the box wider or narrower. Turn it to any angle, or stand it on end for a book's margin.
- A Bluetooth or USB keyboard that KOReader has set up types into it too.

## Pictures

Pick **Image** to add a picture from a file, or search for one online on Wikimedia
or Openverse (Wi-Fi needed). Then move, resize and turn it, or cut it out of its
background. A picture can also sit behind a drawing so you can trace over it.

## Stickers

Ready-made notes, speech bubbles, marks, tape and small pictures.

- **Post-its** come in many shapes (flat, curled, torn, taped or pinned) and in any colour.
- Tap a sticker to put it on the page, ready to move, resize or turn.
- Write on a note with the pen or the Text tool, and the writing moves with it.
- **Mine** holds your own pictures as stickers.
- Hold a sticker to hide it. Hidden ones come back in the sticker options.

The Stickers button is in the book toolbar, and next to Image in the notebook
toolbar. Settings can move it under Image if your toolbar is crowded.
