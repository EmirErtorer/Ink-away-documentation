## Shapes

Lines, curves, single and double arrows, rectangles, ellipses and triangles,
filled or outlined. Shapes can snap to the grid or to 45°.

**Hold to straighten:** you don't always need the Shapes tool. Draw a rough line,
box, ellipse or triangle with the pen and hold the pen still at the end: it snaps
clean. Writing is left alone.

**Paint bucket:** in Shapes, fills a closed area in one tap.

## Eraser

The eraser clears ink back to transparent, or removes whole strokes at once. Tap
it twice to choose.

- It leaves pictures alone unless you tell it otherwise, and can spare text boxes too.
- On a stylus with an eraser end, flip the pen over to erase.
- On a book, it also takes away highlights you made with the highlighter.

## Symmetry

Draw mirrored left to right, top to bottom, or four ways at once. Good for
mandalas, faces and patterns.

## Undo and redo

From the toolbar, or tap with two fingers to undo and double tap with two fingers
to redo.
