## Kinds of pen

| Pen | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| Fineliner | Even lines for notes and line art |
| Ballpoint | Everyday writing |
| Fountain | Writing with a little swell |
| Calligraphy | Lettering, with its own nib angle |
| Pencil | Sketching, with a grainy line |
| Highlighter | Marking. It blends with the page, and text stays black under it |
| Marker | Bold strokes that blend |
| Watercolor | Washes that blend where they overlap |
| Smudge | Mixing what is already on the page, like paint |

Every pen has a size and an opacity. Grey screens get greys, colour screens get
full colour. The **brush maker** in the pen settings makes a pen of your own.

## Pen case

Keep up to 20 pens, each shown at its real size. Your first ones also sit on a
small floating strip, so switching pens is one tap.

## Pen pressure

On a Kindle Scribe and on Kobo readers with a stylus, the line can follow how hard
you press. It is a beta: turn it on in Settings, **Pen and input**.

## Colour

On a colour screen, pick from the palette or the colour wheel. Ink Away's theme
colour (green unless you change it) and colour ink show as you draw. See
[Pen and screen](/docs/devices/pen-and-screen) for dark mode and night mode.
