## Select

Pick **Lasso** and draw a loop around writing, shapes, pictures or text. Then you can:

- move, resize, turn or flip it,
- change its colour, opacity or size,
- duplicate or delete it,
- cut, copy and paste it to any page of any notebook or drawing,
- make it a link to another page (see [Notebooks](/docs/notebooks/notebooks#links-and-contents)).

With **Pan** on, a single touch picks a shape or picture without the lasso.

## Convert to text

1. Lasso some handwriting.
2. Choose **Convert to text**.
3. Check what was read and fix any mistakes.
4. Tap **Convert** to turn it into a text box, or **Cancel** to keep the writing.

The reading happens on the reader itself, with no internet. It knows English
letters and digits. If you change your mind, one undo brings the writing back.

## Convert to text online (experimental)

For handwriting the reader can't read well, you can use an online service of your
own instead.

1. Tap the **Text** tool twice to open its settings.
2. Turn on **Convert to text online**.
3. Fill in **Address**, **Key** and **Model**.

The service has to accept pictures through the OpenAI-style Chat Completions API.
For example, the address `https://your-provider.example/v1` with the model
`paddleocr-vl`.

Good to know:

- Only the writing you selected is sent, as a black-on-white picture. The page, pictures and other writing are not.
- The key is never shown, but it is stored unencrypted in KOReader's settings (not in notebooks or exports).
- Only HTTPS addresses with a valid certificate work. A request can take up to 90 seconds.
- If the service fails, your writing stays as it was.
