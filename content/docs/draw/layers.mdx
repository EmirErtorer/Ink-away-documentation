title: "Layers"
description: "Sketch, ink and colour on separate layers, as in a drawing app."
---

![Layers](/screenshots/layers.gif)

## Turn them on

Tap **File**, then **Layers**. What you drew so far becomes the first layer. You
can have up to five.

Drawings start without layers, so nothing changes until you want them.

## The strip

While layers are on, a small strip shows at the right of the screen.

- **Tap a layer** to draw on it.
- **Tap it again** to show or hide it, rename it, move it, merge it down or delete it.

## How they behave

- A stroke on a lower layer stays under the layers above it while you draw, so you can colour under line art.
- The eraser, the lasso and the paint bucket only touch the layer you are on.
- Hidden layers stay out of exports.
- **Merge down** joins a layer with the one under it. Turning layers off merges them all.

Layers don't slow drawing down or take much memory: no layer is kept as a picture
of its own.
