Ink Away has a public API that other KOReader plugins can use to work with it,
without touching its internals or monkey-patching it.

<Callout type="warn" title="Experimental">
The API ships with Ink Away 4.4.3 and newer. It may change before Ink Away 5.0, so
check the [version numbers](/docs/api/integration-api-version) and do nothing if
they are not the ones you know.
</Callout>

## What you can do

- **Get told when annotation mode opens and closes**, on KOReader's own page or on yours. See the [event-only example](/docs/integration/event-only).
- **Let readers annotate your own view.** If your plugin shows a book's pages its own way, Ink Away's annotation mode can open over your view, on the page it shows. See the [rendering example](/docs/integration/rendering-consumer).

## What the reader sees

- Your viewer gets an "annotate" action, or the reader uses Ink Away's own gesture or menu. Annotation mode opens over your viewer, with Ink Away's toolbar. The drawing area is the page's part of the screen; your controls and margins stay out of it.
- Page turns in annotation mode ask your viewer to turn (it may animate). On a right-to-left page (manga), the toolbar's left arrow goes forward.
- Closing annotation mode keeps the ink, and your viewer paints it in its own view from then on.
- The ink is stored exactly as if it had been drawn in KOReader's own view, so it shows there too, syncs, and exports like any other.

## Example: Panels+

<video width="30%" controls height="auto" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fdf3ef83-c7df-48f4-bb30-ca32a2305b90"></video>

[Panels+](https://github.com/KristanLaimon/PanelsPlus) reads comics panel by panel
with its own renderer. To let readers draw on a panel, it needs to:

- tell Ink Away where the page is on its screen, so the ink lands on the right spot of the real page,
- turn to its next or previous panel when the toolbar's `>` or `<` is pressed.

The API was designed by [Kristan Laimon](https://github.com/KristanLaimon) for
exactly that.

## Good to know

- Nothing changes for anyone not using a viewer plugin: KOReader's own page works exactly as before.
- Every call to your surface is guarded. A failure is logged and passed to your `on_error`, and annotation mode falls back to KOReader's page or closes, keeping the ink.
- Only fixed-page books (PDF, comics, DjVu) can be shown through a surface.
- The source of truth is [docs/INTEGRATION.md](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/blob/main/docs/INTEGRATION.md) in the Ink Away repository, with Lua language server types in [integration-types.lua](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/blob/main/docs/integration-types.lua).
