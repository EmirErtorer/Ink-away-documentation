Ink Away is a KOReader plugin. It turns your e-reader into a sketchbook, a notebook
and a pen for your books, with **palm rejection** and **pen pressure** for stylus
users. It works with a finger too.

![Writing on a book with Ink Away](/screenshots/book-annotate.gif)

## What it does

- **Draw** with pens of every kind, watercolor and smudge, on up to five layers.
- **Take notes** in notebooks of lined, grid, dotted or planner pages, and turn handwriting into text.
- **Write on any book** KOReader opens, from EPUB to PDF. On a reflowing book the ink follows its words when you change the font.
- **Take it with you.** Export as PDF or PNG, send files to your phone over Wi-Fi, or sync with Dropbox or Google Drive.

## Works on

Kindle, Kobo, Boox and other Android readers, reMarkable, and the other readers
KOReader runs on. Colour screens get colour ink. Palm rejection needs KOReader
2026.07 or newer.

Ink Away is free and open source, under the MIT license. The code, releases and
issue tracker are on [GitHub](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin).
