---
title: "Send over Wi-Fi"
description: "Move files between your reader and a phone or computer with a QR code."
---

No cable, no app, no account. Your reader serves a small page on your home Wi-Fi,
and nothing leaves your network.

## Send to your phone or computer

1. Turn on the reader's Wi-Fi.
2. Open **Export** and tap **Send to phone or computer (Wi-Fi)**.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone, or type the address it shows into a browser on a computer. Both must be on the same Wi-Fi.
4. Download your notebooks and drawings as PDF, PNG or Ink Away files.

## Get files from your phone or computer

On the same page, send:

- **PDFs**, which become notebooks,
- **pictures**, which can be made smaller on the phone first if you like.

They arrive in **Library**, **Imported**. When adding a picture to a page, **Image**
also has **Get from your phone or computer**.

## If the page doesn't load

- Check the phone or computer is on the same Wi-Fi as the reader, not on mobile data or a guest network.
- On a Mac or PC, the firewall can block it. Allow the browser, or try the phone.
