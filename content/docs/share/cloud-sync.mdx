---
title: "Cloud sync"
description: "Keep your notebooks and book ink the same on several readers, with Dropbox or Google Drive."
---

Ink Away syncs through **your own** Dropbox or Google Drive account, and only in
Ink Away's own folder there.

## Connect

1. Open **Cloud sync**: in the library's **...** menu, or the cloud button in the library's top bar, in Settings, or in the **Book ink** settings.
2. Pick Dropbox or Google Drive.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone and tap **Allow**.
4. **Dropbox:** paste the code it gives you on the page the reader shows on your Wi-Fi (or type it on the reader). **Google Drive:** enter the short code on your phone.

## What syncs

You choose, part by part:

| Part | Starts |
| --- | --- |
| Notebooks and drawings | On |
| Book notes | On |
| Ink on book pages | Off |
| Imported PDFs and pictures | Off |
| Exports | Off |
| Screen clips | Off |
| Pens, colours and preferences | Off |

A part that is off is left alone on both sides. Ink on a book is matched across
readers by the book's contents, whatever the file is called. KOReader's own
highlights are not synced: use KOReader's own sync for those.

## When it syncs

After closing a notebook, after a pause in drawing, on a timer, when the reader
sleeps, or only when you tap **Sync now**. It runs in the background. Wi-Fi is
turned on by itself only if you allow it.

Syncing by itself can slow the reader and use battery. If it does, choose **Manual
only** and use **Sync now**.

## It never loses work

- Before anything is replaced or removed, this reader's copy goes to a backup folder for 30 days.
- A file changed on two readers is kept twice, and you settle it from the **Conflicts** list.
- A deletion never wins over an edit.
- Earlier versions of a notebook can be brought back.

If something goes wrong, the message has a code (like IA-S101) that says what
happened and what to do, and the sync log has the details.
