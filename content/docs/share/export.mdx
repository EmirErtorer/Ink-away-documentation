---
title: "Export"
description: "PNG, PDF and editable PDF, from a drawing, a notebook or a book."
---

Open **File**, then **Export**. In a book, Export is in the **Book ink** settings, or in
KOReader's **Tools**, **Ink Away**. Exports go to `ink away/exports` unless you pick
another folder.

## PNG

- Transparent or white background, at your screen size.
- The whole page, or an area you pick. With or without the grid.
- From a notebook or a book: the page shown, all pages, only the pages with ink, or a range. Each page is its own PNG, in a folder of their own.

## PDF

A notebook, some of its pages, or a whole folder at once. It has bookmarks for
titled pages, working links and page numbers, on white or sandpaper paper.

## Editable PDF

For a PDF notebook, or a PDF you annotate while reading. You get a copy of the
original with your ink and highlights added as real PDF annotations:

- the text stays selectable,
- the file stays about the size it was,
- any PDF app can edit or remove your marks.

Some things can't be PDF annotations (text boxes, pictures, fills). Ink Away lists
them first, and you choose: leave them out, or keep those pages' ink as a small
picture over just those pages, while every other page stays editable.

## A book as a PDF

Saves an EPUB, MOBI or any other book as a fixed PDF, laid out as you are reading it
now, with your strokes, shapes, pictures and highlights exactly where they are, in
your colours. Each page keeps its text under the picture, so you can search, select
and copy it in any PDF app.

You can also save only the pages with ink as PNGs, or only your ink on white pages.
A progress bar shows how far it is, and **Stop** ends the export and deletes what
it wrote.
